# BVSE — Practical Assessment

Not a published source. My own surface-level observation.

---

BVSE is good at **ranking** materials — distinguishing good ionic conductors from poor ones — but is not reliable for predicting **absolute conductivity values**.

This makes it useful as a screening filter (eliminate clearly bad candidates, identify promising classes) but not as a substitute for experimental measurement or higher-fidelity simulation (AIMD) when you need a number.
